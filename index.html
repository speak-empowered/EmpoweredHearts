OUR PURPOSE

No parent should have to choose between financial stability and their child's care.

When a child's health becomes uncertain, families can face overwhelming medical bills, ongoing treatment costs, long-term care expenses and lost income. For many low- and middle-income households, that stress can become nearly as heavy as the illness itself.

Through fundraising and the generosity of compassionate donors, Empowered Hearts Foundation works to provide direct relief that helps families focus on what matters most: caring for their child.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The medical crisis is only part of the burden.

Families navigating serious childhood illness can face financial pressure from several directions at once.

Medical Costs

Even with insurance, hospital stays and complex care can create substantial out-of-pocket expenses.

Lost Income

Caregiving demands can force parents to reduce work hours or leave jobs, adding another layer of financial strain.

Long-Term Pressure

Families dealing with disability, chronic conditions or long-term treatment may carry financial burdens long after the initial diagnosis.

Nearly 2× Families of children with disabilities are nearly twice as likely to report difficulty paying medical bills.
$1,300–$3,000+ Potential out-of-pocket cost per pediatric hospital admission, depending on coverage and circumstances.

For nearly 25 years, Nathan has helped leaders and teams unlock their potential, build healthier cultures and create workplaces where people feel valued and supported. His experience spans corporate retail, higher education and small business.

Along the way, he learned something bigger: leadership isn't just about performance — it's about people. That belief fueled the creation of Empowered Hearts Foundation, extending his mission beyond the workplace to families facing severe childhood illness and overwhelming medical costs.
